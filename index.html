How this MVP decides

ALLER-AI does not ask an LLM to decide whether a product is safe. It normalizes multilingual ingredient terms, maps known derivatives to allergen groups, checks precautionary phrases such as “may contain,” and then applies deterministic rules.

Research/demo prototype only. Ingredient labels can be incomplete, OCR can be wrong, formulations can change, and cross-contact risks may not be fully represented. For severe allergies, verify the original package label and manufacturer information.
